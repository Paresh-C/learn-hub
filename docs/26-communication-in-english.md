## 26. Why communication in English matters

- **Software runs on English.** Documentation, library APIs, error messages, GitHub/GitLab issues, Stack Overflow, RFCs, release notes, conference talks and most tutorials are in English. Reading faster means learning faster.
- **Code is written in English.** Identifiers, commit messages, comments, branch names and API fields use English words; consistent naming makes shared code readable by everyone.
- **International teams and remote work** use English as the common language, in writing and in meetings. It widens your job market and your network.
- **Open source contribution** (issues, MRs, reviews) happens in English.
- **Career growth:** interviews, promotions and technical leadership depend on explaining your thinking clearly to people who do not share your first language.
- **AI tools:** prompts, error messages and documentation work best and are easiest to verify in English.
- **Fewer misunderstandings:** a clear written message beats a long discussion.

**Practical ways to improve**

- **Write simply:** short sentences, active voice, one idea per sentence, no idioms. Clarity beats sophistication.
- **Write first, then speak:** prepare standup updates and meeting points in writing; later you will need fewer notes.
- **Daily micro-practice:** write commit messages, MR descriptions and time reports in careful English; read a doc or an article daily; keep a phrase bank of expressions you hear.
- **Use AI as a tutor:** ask it to correct your message and explain the changes. Check that the corrected message still says what you mean.
- **Shadowing and recording:** repeat meeting phrases aloud; record a 1-minute update and listen to it.
- **Do not fear accents or errors.** Ask for clarification without apology; clarity matters more than perfection.

**Useful phrases**

| Situation | Phrase |
| --- | --- |
| Clarify | Could you clarify what you mean by...? / Just to confirm, you want X, right? |
| Buy time | Let me check and get back to you by end of day. |
| Disagree politely | I see it differently. My concern is...; What if we...? |
| Offer options | I see two options: A is faster, B is safer. I recommend B because... |
| Report a blocker | I'm blocked on X. I need Y from Z by Thursday to continue. |
| Report progress | I finished A, I'm working on B, and C is next. |
| Ask for help | I tried A and B and got this error. Do you have an idea where to look? |
| Confirm action | I'll update the issue and send a summary today. |
| Missed it | Sorry, could you repeat the last part? |
